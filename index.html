Void Graze — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy covers the Android game Void Graze ("the app"), published on Google Play by CG92 Studios ("I", "me").

Summary

The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no internet access.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your scores, stars, unlocked ships and upgrades, pilot rank, trophies, mission progress, a replay of your best daily-challenge run, and your settings on your own device. This information never leaves your device, and I cannot access it. If Android's backup is turned on, the system may include this data in your personal Google device backup. That backup is controlled by your Google account settings, not by me.

Permissions

The app requests only the VIBRATE permission, for haptic feedback. You can turn vibration off in the game.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks or analytics services.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will publish the updated version at this address and change the date above.
